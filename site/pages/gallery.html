    Voting rules

    • Anyone can add an app. Paste its public link above. Each link can be listed only once.
    • One vote per app. Vote for as many apps as you like, but only once each. A vote can't be taken back.
    • Not your own. You can't vote for an app you added.
    • Anonymous. The list shows only totals. No names are stored, and nobody can see who voted for which app.
    • One browser, one voter. There's no sign-in: your browser keeps a random key that stands for you. Please add and vote from one browser, and don't vote again from another device or a private window.
