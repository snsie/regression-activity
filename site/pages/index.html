Explain the Model

Build one app that explains three regression models {{help regression}} to someone who has never heard of machine learning. Your classmates try your app and vote for the best explainers.

You, working on your own with an AI coding agent

Publish

Your GitHub repository link on Canvas, with your app link at the top of its README
Win
Peer votes: one winner for each model

Start here

  1. Get the starter. It already has all three sections, a dataset menu, a menu of five example people from the data, and the correct numbers in plain tables. Data & files
  2. Publish a test link before class. Put the starter on GitHub and on a free host, then open it in a private window. Publish & back up
  3. Redesign each section into an explanation a newcomer would understand. You never train a model; the numbers are supplied {{help model-outputs}}.
  4. Check, push, submit on Canvas. Submission checklist

New to these ideas?

Look for the ? buttons. Each one opens a short, plain-language explanation of the term next to it. The glossary lists them all.

Fair play

  • Use the supplied numbers. Don't retrain the models or invent predictions.
  • Work on your own. Your AI agent writes code; you make the design decisions and check the numbers.
  • Back up as you go. Commit and push to GitHub whenever a section works.
  • Freeze it after you submit. Don't change your app during evaluation unless your instructor approves a fix.
